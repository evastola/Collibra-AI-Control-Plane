How I run every program

The method is the same for each one: spend the time on the real problem, get the right people to the answer, and keep it moving.

DefineOne-page brief: who is affected, the evidence, the measure.
ConveneThe six or seven right people, one owner per piece.
DecideOne option, a decision log, an owner and a date.
DeliverOwners do the work. I track risks, dependencies and one status view.

Program 2

Make every launch sellable on day one

Launch gates done
10 daysRelease to first quote, from 21 (sample)
60%Launches ready on day one (sample)

A launch is not done until a seller can quote it, demo it and explain it. Make “ready to sell” a sequence of gates with owners, then make the change stick in the seller’s workflow instead of another training.

Ready-to-sell gates, in the order they have to happen (tap to change status)

After launch: a 30-day review sends what sellers and customers are saying back to Product, and what just shipped back to sellers. Acquired capabilities, like those from Deasy Labs and trail ML, go through the same gates so they become plays sellers can use, not separate motions.

Feedback travels up to Product, and what ships travels back down to sellers. Both directions need an owner and a cadence.

Two numbers I would report: days from feedback to a product decision, and days from release to first quote.
